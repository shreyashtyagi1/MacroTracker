Data sources & accuracy

Packaged-food lookup uses Open Food Facts, a community-built database. Available nutrients vary by product and region. Calories and macros may be listed per 100 g, per serving, or missing; this app uses per-100 g values and lets you choose the amount eaten.

Open Food Facts data is provided as-is. Always verify with the nutrition panel on the actual pack.
